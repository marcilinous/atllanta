The honest problem

Five tools pretending to be one company.

A hiring tool, an attendance app, an HR sheet, a CRM, and a WhatsApp group holding it together. Nothing talks. Atllanta is the other way.

The five-app stack

  • A separate bill for every function
  • Data re-typed between apps
  • Five logins, five support queues
  • Priced in dollars, built elsewhere

Atllanta

  • One platform for hiring, people & customers
  • One record every module reads from
  • One login, one inbox, one search
  • Rupees, IST, Apr–Mar out of the box
What's inside

Everything to run people & sales.

Not add-ons or upsells — these ship in the box, from day one.

AI recruitment

Parse resumes against a JD, rank candidates with a transparent score, shortlist and schedule interviews — without the enterprise ATS bill.

Attendance

Selfie + geofenced check-in, regularization and reports — accurate even when your team is out in the field.

Leave

Balances, holidays, apply and approve — in one inbox managers actually check.

CRM & pipeline

Accounts, contacts, leads and a weighted deal pipeline for your sales team.

Approvals & audit

One approvals inbox, a full audit trail, and clear owner / admin / manager / member roles.

AI assistant

Ask questions across your data — permission-aware, so it never shows what a person can't see.

Ready to use

Installable app

Works offline

Check in and log visits with no signal — everything syncs on reconnect.

Isolated by organization

Every org's data is walled off at the database level. Your team sees only your data.
